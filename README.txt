🎬 CHENNAI CINEMA TICKET MONITOR (BMS + DISTRICT)
================================================================

Monitors BookMyShow and District across all dates in Chennai for:
  • Avengers: Doomsday
  • Dune: Part Three

When tickets or new dates open:
  • Laptop speaks out loud through speakers ("Alert! New theatres opened...")
  • Warning audio chime plays
  • On-screen popup box appears over all windows with a "Book Now" button
  • Pushes alerts to phone via the free ntfy app (Optional)

----------------------------------------------------------------
🧪 HOW TO TEST YOUR NOTIFICATIONS FIRST:
----------------------------------------------------------------

• On Mac:
  Double-click "TEST_ALERTS_MAC.command"

• On Windows:
  Double-click "TEST_ALERTS_WINDOWS.bat"

(You will immediately hear the voice, audio chime, and see the popup!)

----------------------------------------------------------------
🚀 HOW TO START LIVE MONITORING:
----------------------------------------------------------------

• On Mac:
  Double-click "START_MAC.command"
  *(If Mac says "unidentified developer", right-click -> Open -> Open).*

• On Windows:
  Double-click "START_WINDOWS.bat"

----------------------------------------------------------------
📱 PHONE NOTIFICATIONS (Optional):
----------------------------------------------------------------
To get alerts on your phone:
1. Install the free "ntfy" app from App Store / Google Play.
2. In the app, subscribe to: https://ntfy.sh/nandy-bms-avengers-2026
3. Whenever tickets open, your phone rings automatically!
